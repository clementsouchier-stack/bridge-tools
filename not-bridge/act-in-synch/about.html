WHO WE ARE

Music, culture and action — across Europe.

Act in Synch is a platform connecting music, culture and social innovation to explore how creativity can help shape a more sustainable and connected world. It brings together artists, changemakers and industry leaders through summits, performances and collaborations across Europe.

EUROPE IN SYNCH

Part of a broader European cooperation project.

Act in Synch is the annual event of Europe in Synch. Europe in Synch brings together partners from across Europe to build knowledge, networks and better practices around synchronisation, music licensing and music supervision. Act in Synch is presented by Europe in Synch and co-funded by the European Union through the Creative Europe programme.

Culture as infrastructure

Music is approached not only as entertainment, but as a catalyst for connection, innovation and action.

Cross-sector by design

Music, film, art, design, fashion, gaming, technology, science and culture are deliberately brought into the same conversation.

From awareness to action

The platform is designed to turn discussion into collaboration across creative and social ecosystems.
