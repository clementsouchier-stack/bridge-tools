Why this matters

A rights issue can outlive the production that created it.

Commercial exploitation depends on a clear chain of title. If the origin or legal status of a generated asset is unclear, the consequences can extend to the campaign, film, series or game in which it is used.

01

Advertising

Campaign interruption, media waste, re-editing and redelivery.

02

Clearance, E&O, warranty and distribution implications.

03

Long-tail exposure

How the risk arises

AI-generated does not automatically mean rights-cleared.

Commercial productions depend on a documented chain of title. Uncertainty around training data, model terms or output rights can move into the finished campaign, film, series or game.

01

Training data

Protected works may be present in model training data.

02

Generative model

The legal basis for training differs by provider and jurisdiction.

03

Generated asset

Ownership, provenance and similarity may require verification.

04

Commercial production

A later rights claim can affect already-distributed content.

A crucial distinction

“Commercial use permitted” does not mean “rights cleared”.

A platform’s permission to use an output commercially is a contractual permission from that platform. It is not independent confirmation that underlying rights are cleared, that the output is non-infringing, or that third-party claims are covered.

Why the risk can persist over time

Not detected today does not mean undetectable tomorrow.

Detection, watermarking and provenance technologies continue to improve. Material released today can be re-analysed later, which makes documentation and contractual clarity important over the full exploitation period.

What responsible use looks like

AI is not the problem. Unclear rights are.

Responsible creative AI can exist when provenance, permissions, contracts and creator interests are documented and understood before commercial use.

01

Responsible AI

Licensed or authorised sources, transparent provenance, clear commercial terms and identifiable responsibility.

02

Unclear AI

Unknown training sources, weak warranties, uncertain chain of title and risk transferred downstream.

03

Human-created music

Identifiable creators and rights holders, established licensing pathways and a documentable chain of title.
